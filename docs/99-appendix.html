Appendix A — Methods, decisions and references

Within-sample diversity

For counts \(x_i\), \(p_i=x_i/\sum_j x_j\) and \(H=-\sum_{i:p_i>0}p_i\ln(p_i)\). Observed richness is the number of positive counts; evenness concerns how abundance is distributed. Shannon is not a health score, and its interpretation depends on the feature definition.

Depth affects detection. Rarefaction curves show expected detected features in smaller samples drawn from the already processed table. They cannot recover discarded features or prove complete biological sampling. The Zackular object has undergone upstream processing; interpret its curves within that limitation. The initial synthetic example has only 15 fictional features, so rapid saturation is a property of the teaching data.

Depth 0 in settings means the smallest supplied sample total. That is a convenience, not a universal recommendation. Inspect low-depth samples first. For Zackular, 15,000 retains every sample. Choose and justify a depth before looking for a favourable group result; record excluded samples if needed.

Community composition: PCoA, PERMANOVA and dispersion

Bray–Curtis quantifies compositional dissimilarity. PCoA places samples in a low-dimensional representation of that distance. Axes can reverse signs between implementations without changing relationships. No phylogenetic tree is supplied, so this workflow does not calculate UniFrac.

PCoA uses Lingoes-corrected distances. PERMANOVA and dispersion use the same correction. PERMANOVA uses marginal tests (by = "margin"), so the group term is conditional on configured covariates. Its R-squared reports the fraction of total distance variation attributed to the term; it is not classification accuracy. A significant dispersion result can complicate a location-based interpretation. A nonsignificant result does not prove equal dispersion. The separate dispersion test does not adjust for covariates.

Both proportion-based and equal-depth sensitivity results are saved. A change in conclusion is evidence to investigate, not a reason to select the smaller p-value. The sensitivity averages distances across random subsamples; it is not a pooled meta-analysis of repeated tests. Do not compare PCoA axis positions numerically between different distance matrices.

These scripts assume independent observations. Paired samples, repeated visits, shared cages, households and clustered recruitment can require a different model and restricted permutations. Setting design_confirmed to TRUE does not make that assumption correct.

OTU differential abundance

ANCOM-BC2 estimates model-based abundance associations from supplied counts while correcting sampling biases under its assumptions. It does not measure absolute bacterial load. The guide tests OTUs; a genus label is an annotation, not genus-level aggregation.

The Zackular model is age + sex + collection_location + group. Group is internally coded Reference then Comparison. A positive group coefficient means higher estimated abundance in Carcinoma versus Healthy, conditional on those covariates. Effects use natural logarithms, so exp(effect) is a model-estimated fold change; ln(2) is approximately a twofold association. These are not DESeq2’s log2 effects.

Features detected in at least 10% of the 60 samples enter the feature model. BH correction addresses multiple feature tests for each coefficient. A highlighted discovery must have adjusted p < 0.05, the package’s DA indicator and passage through pseudo-count sensitivity (conservative = TRUE). Unavailable effects or standard errors are explicitly flagged with estimate_available = FALSE; their learner-facing p-values are left missing. The package’s original coefficient table is also retained. ANCOMBC may assign p = q = 1 to an unestimable test; that is not evidence for a null effect. Unavailable results are never discoveries. Structural-zero detection is disabled in this example; no manual pseudo-count is added to model inputs. The display offset is unrelated.

Approximate pointwise 95% intervals are effect ± 1.96 standard errors. They are not simultaneous intervals and do not replace adjusted p-values. The forest display ranks up to 20 features by adjusted p-value even when none is a discovery. Do not interpret its ranking as validation of biomarkers.

Only 15 features exist in synthetic practice, and their generating truth is not a validated DA benchmark. Its default inference demonstrates community tests; ANCOM-BC2 is taught with the larger human table. Your own study starts with inference blocked and DA disabled until you justify the design.

Human dataset provenance

Zackular JP et al. (2014), The Human Gut Microbiome as a Screening Tool for Colorectal Cancer. Study. Original study: 90 stools, 30 per clinical group, 16S V4 MiSeq. Original metadata and sequences. The packaged microbiomeutilities::zackular2014 object contains 88 samples and 2,078 OTUs. All sample IDs, disease labels and ages were checked against the original spreadsheet. Two adenoma records are absent, reason unresolved. All 30 healthy and all 30 carcinoma records are retained here.

Its package documentation cites a different 2013 mouse study; use the human citation above. Exact sources and checksums are in data/provenance/zackular/. The spreadsheet’s participant-ID field is empty. Sample-name tokens are unique and consistent with the study design, but are not a validated participant crosswalk. Do not claim a separate participant identity audit.

The exported sex labels were trimmed (including nonbreaking whitespace) and mapped f→Female, m→Male; Ann Arbor,. MI was harmonised to Ann Arbor, MI. Original labels are retained alongside cleaned labels in metadata. These changes were made before modelling, not chosen to improve test results. BMI, medications and other possible confounders are not included in this teaching model. Conclusions remain observational and sample-specific.

What is outside this practical?

Raw-read QC, denoising, contamination assessment, reference-based taxonomy, phylogenetic tree building, functional prediction, microbial networks and predictive-model validation each require additional evidence and workflows.

Numerical limitations in the supplied human run

The ANCOMBC t-distribution calculation emitted NaNs produced. The exported summary flags 100 OTUs without finite group estimates or standard errors; all 100 have rank-deficient design matrices when restricted to samples with positive counts. feature-model-support.tsv makes that support visible. Do not interpret the package’s replacement p-values of 1 for unavailable tests as evidence of equality. Other estimable results must still pass the pseudo-count sensitivity criterion. The two-group warning about deactivating multi-group tests is expected: no global or pairwise multi-group analysis was requested. Read both warnings rather than silencing them without explanation.
